Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NGOF Media Manager handles information when interacting with TikTok and other social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to operate the service:

  • TikTok account ID
  • Access tokens provided by TikTok
  • Media files voluntarily uploaded by authorized users

2. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively to authenticate accounts and publish media content on behalf of authorized NGO accounts.

3. Data Storage

We do not sell or share user data. Access tokens are stored securely and are accessible only by the application backend.

4. Third-Party Services

This application uses TikTok Business APIs. TikTok may collect additional information according to their own Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights

Users may request deletion of stored data at any time by contacting the NGOF IT Department.

6. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in platform policies or legal requirements.

Social Media API Usage Policy

1. Policy Statement

This policy governs the responsible use of social media APIs for posting content and storing limited post-related data for organizational purposes.

2. Authorized Use

  • Posting official content to NGO-owned social media accounts
  • Storing limited, non-personal posting data for documentation and reporting

3. Prohibited Use

  • Collecting or storing personal user data
  • Accessing private messages or comments for profiling
  • Commercial exploitation of data
  • Posting content without approval

4. Data Protection and Storage

  • Only minimal data required for operations shall be stored
  • Data must be stored securely with access controls
  • No data sharing without authorization

5. Content Governance

All content must be reviewed and approved by the Communications Team and must comply with NGO values, safeguarding principles, and platform policies.

6. Compliance and Monitoring

API usage may be monitored internally. Any breach must be reported immediately.

7. Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action or suspension of API access.

8. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually or when platform or legal changes occur.
