Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NGOF Media Manager handles user information when interacting with TikTok and other social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process only the information needed to authenticate users and upload media content, including:

  • TikTok account ID
  • Access tokens provided by TikTok
  • Media files voluntarily uploaded by the user

2. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively for posting media content on behalf of authorized users.

3. Data Storage

We do not share or sell user data. Access tokens are stored securely and accessible only to the authorized application backend.

4. Third-Party Services

We use TikTok Business APIs to authenticate accounts and upload videos. TikTok may collect additional data according to their Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights

Users may request removal of stored data at any time by contacting NGOF IT Department.

6. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page.
