Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The NGO Forum on Cambodia handles information in connection with NGOF Media Manager, including its TikTok integration.

1. Who operates the service

NGOF Media Manager is an organizational media management platform operated for The NGO Forum on Cambodia. It is intended for authorized organizational users and official social media accounts.

2. Information processed

Depending on the enabled integration and actions taken by an authorized user, the service may process:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information made available through the authorized TikTok integration.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Media files, captions and related metadata selected by an authorized user for publishing.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

3. How information is used

  • To authenticate and maintain an authorized connection to a social media account.
  • To provide requested media management and publishing functions.
  • To maintain records of organizational publishing activity.
  • To secure, maintain and troubleshoot the application.

4. TikTok authorization

When a user connects TikTok, the user is redirected to TikTok to sign in and authorize the requested permissions. The application receives the authorization response through its registered server callback. NGOF Media Manager does not ask users to provide their TikTok password to the application.

5. Tokens and security

Access and refresh tokens are handled by the application backend and are not intended to be exposed in the public website or client-side source code. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect stored information.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested integration, comply with applicable law, protect the service, or respond to a lawful request.

7. Third-party platforms

The service integrates with third-party platforms, including TikTok. Those platforms process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review the relevant platform policies before authorizing an integration.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, security, reporting or legal purposes. An authorized user may request deletion of information associated with an integration by contacting the NGOF IT team.

9. Privacy requests

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact info@ngoforum.org.kh.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, integrations, organizational requirements or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
