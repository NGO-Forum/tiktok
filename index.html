NGOF Media Manager

NGOF Media Manager is an internal web-based platform developed for The NGO Forum on Cambodia to manage and publish official media content to social media platforms such as TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

What We Do

  • Upload and publish approved media content
  • Manage official NGO social media accounts
  • Support advocacy and public awareness campaigns

Who Can Use This System

Only authorized staff members of The NGO Forum on Cambodia may access this system.
